# Audio credits

All recorded audio is CC0 (public domain) from OpenGameArt. Clips were trimmed, faded and re-encoded.

- `trex-roar.m4a` — a 3 second excerpt (the final roar) of "T-Rex Calls" by CaveboyTup
  (https://opengameart.org/content/t-rex-calls).
- `step-land-*.m4a`, `step-stone-*.m4a` — from "Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)", submitted by qubodup
  (https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone).
- `step-water-*.m4a` — from "40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX" by rubberduck
  (https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx).

Everything else you hear in the game is synthesised in code.
